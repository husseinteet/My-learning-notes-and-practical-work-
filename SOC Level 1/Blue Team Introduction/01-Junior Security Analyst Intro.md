# Junior Security Analyst Intro

**Platform:** TryHackMe | **Path:** SOC Level 1

---

## The Role

A Junior Security Analyst sits at the entry point of a SOC team. The core job is simple: watch the alerts, investigate what looks suspicious, and escalate what you can't handle alone. You are not expected to know everything — but you are expected to be the first to notice when something is wrong.

---

## What the Day Looks Like

The shift starts with catching up on the latest cyber news. Breaches happen daily, threat groups are always active, and new vulnerabilities keep emerging. Knowing what is happening outside your organization helps you understand what to watch for inside it.

During the shift, the main tasks are:
- Reviewing and investigating security alerts
- Working through open tickets before they pile up
- Collaborating with teammates when a case needs a second opinion
- Keeping up with new attack techniques and defenses

---

## The People Around You

A SOC is never one person. Understanding who does what matters:

| Role | What They Actually Do |
|---|---|
| Senior Analyst | Your go-to when a case gets complex or unclear |
| SOC Engineer | Builds and maintains the tools you use every day |
| SOC Manager | Handles reporting and keeps the team on track |
| Incident Responder | Only appears when something serious is happening |

---

## What You Will Eventually Handle

As you grow in the role, the cases get more interesting:
- Catching malware quietly stealing data from an employee's machine
- Tracing a phishing campaign before it reaches the finance team
- Being part of the response to a ransomware attack
- Helping build the detection rules that catch the next threat

---

## Lab

Accessed the internal alert dashboard, identified a malicious IP address in the active alerts, escalated the case to the senior analyst, and confirmed the IP was blocked at the firewall level.

---

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SOC | Security Operations Center |
| Alert | A notification flagging suspicious activity |
| Ticket | An assigned alert or task that needs resolution |
| Escalation | Handing a case to someone with more authority or expertise |
| Incident Responder | Specialist brought in for large-scale incidents |
