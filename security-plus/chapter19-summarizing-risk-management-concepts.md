# Security+ Chapter 19 – Summarizing Risk Management Concepts

## Main Idea

This chapter explains how organizations identify, assess, and manage cybersecurity risks. Effective risk management helps minimize threats, protect business assets, and support informed security decisions.

---

## Risk Management

Risk management is the process of identifying, evaluating, and reducing risks that could impact an organization's assets.

### Objectives

- Protect organizational assets
- Reduce security risks
- Support business continuity
- Improve decision-making
- Meet compliance requirements

---

## Key Risk Terminology

### Asset

Anything valuable to an organization.

**Examples:**
- Data
- Servers
- Applications
- Employees
- Intellectual property

### Threat

Anything capable of causing harm to an asset.

**Examples:**
- Hackers
- Malware
- Natural disasters
- Insider threats

### Vulnerability

A weakness that can be exploited by a threat.

**Examples:**
- Unpatched software
- Weak passwords
- Misconfigured systems

### Risk

The likelihood that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause damage.

---

## Risk Assessment

Risk assessment identifies and evaluates potential security risks.

### Steps

1. Identify assets
2. Identify threats
3. Identify vulnerabilities
4. Analyze potential impact
5. Determine risk level
6. Recommend security controls

---

## Types of Risk Assessments

### Qualitative Risk Assessment

Uses descriptive ratings.

**Examples:**

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

### Quantitative Risk Assessment

Uses numerical values to estimate financial loss.

**Benefits:**

- Supports cost analysis
- Assists management decisions
- Measures financial impact

---

## Risk Response Strategies

Organizations choose how to manage identified risks.

### Risk Acceptance

Accept the risk when its impact is minimal or mitigation costs are too high.

### Risk Avoidance

Eliminate activities that introduce unacceptable risks.

### Risk Mitigation

Implement security controls to reduce risk.

**Examples:**

- Firewalls
- Encryption
- Security awareness training

### Risk Transfer

Transfer financial responsibility to another party.

**Examples:**

- Cyber insurance
- Outsourcing

---

## Risk Register

A Risk Register documents identified risks and their current status.

### Typical Contents

- Risk description
- Asset affected
- Likelihood
- Business impact
- Risk owner
- Mitigation plan
- Current status

---

## Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

A BIA identifies the impact of disruptions on business operations.

### Objectives

- Identify critical systems
- Estimate financial losses
- Define recovery priorities
- Support disaster recovery planning

---

## Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance

### Risk Appetite

The amount of risk an organization is willing to accept.

### Risk Tolerance

The acceptable variation around specific business objectives.

---

## Third-Party Risk

Organizations should evaluate risks associated with vendors and service providers.

### Considerations

- Security controls
- Compliance requirements
- Vendor reputation
- Contract agreements
- Incident response capabilities

---

## Security Controls

Security controls reduce organizational risk.

### Administrative Controls

- Policies
- Procedures
- Security awareness training

### Technical Controls

- Firewalls
- Antivirus
- Encryption
- MFA

### Physical Controls

- Locks
- CCTV
- Security guards
- Access badges

---

## Compliance and Governance

Organizations must comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

### Common Standards

- ISO 27001
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- PCI DSS
- HIPAA
- GDPR

---

## Continuous Risk Monitoring

Risk management is an ongoing process.

### Monitoring Activities

- Vulnerability assessments
- Security audits
- Penetration testing
- Continuous monitoring
- Risk reviews

---

## Risk Management Best Practices

- Perform regular risk assessments
- Keep risk registers updated
- Apply layered security controls
- Review vendor security regularly
- Monitor emerging threats
- Conduct security awareness training
- Update business continuity plans

---

## Key Takeaway

Effective risk management enables organizations to identify threats, evaluate vulnerabilities, and implement appropriate security controls. Continuous assessment, monitoring, and informed decision-making help reduce cybersecurity risks and strengthen overall organizational resilience.
